About SteamApp

Steam market intelligence, organized from context to execution.

SteamApp helps operators and analysts connect market context, reusable matching rules, live checks, and automated queues in one focused workspace.

Built for repeatable market intelligence

The platform keeps catalog setup, matching logic, queue execution, monitoring, and run history together so decisions remain repeatable and explainable.

Mission

Reduce the repeated setup behind Steam market research.

SteamApp turns scattered Steam market checks into an operational flow. Instead of rebuilding context every time, teams can define market sources, capture the rules behind a decision, run them directly or in a queue, and keep high-value targets visible over time.
